# Orders, delivery and policies

## Ordering cakes
Celebration cakes must be ordered at least 72 hours in advance by WhatsApp or email. A 50% deposit confirms the order; the balance is paid on collection.

## Delivery
We deliver within 8 km of the bakery on Tuesday to Saturday. Delivery costs R60 and is free on orders over R500. We do not deliver on Sundays.

## Cancellations and refunds
Cake orders cancelled more than 48 hours before collection get a full deposit refund. Cancellations within 48 hours forfeit the deposit. If you are unhappy with a product, bring it back the same day with your receipt for a replacement or refund.

## Payment
We accept cash, card and SnapScan. We do not accept cheques.

## Dietary information
We label products containing nuts. Our vegan range is the rye and caraway loaf and the seeded wholewheat loaf.
