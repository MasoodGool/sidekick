# Opening hours and location

Sixth Street Bakery is at 14 Sixth Street, Melville, Johannesburg. Street parking is available on Sixth Street and Fourth Avenue.

## Opening hours
- Tuesday to Friday: 07:00 to 16:00
- Saturday: 07:30 to 14:00
- Sunday: 08:00 to 12:00
- Monday: closed

On public holidays we open from 08:00 to 12:00, except Christmas Day and New Year's Day, when we are closed.
